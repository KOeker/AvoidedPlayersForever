# Avoided Players Forever

<img src="Media/Logo.png" alt="Avoided Players Forever logo" width="128">

Keep an account-wide avoid list in World of Warcraft: Forever. Save a reason for each player, use WoW’s built-in Ignore list, and hide or highlight avoided characters in the Group Finder.

---

## 🛡️ Core Features

- **Account-wide avoid list** — Search by character name or reason. Your notes are saved locally.
- **Social window integration** — Manage your list from the red X tab alongside Friends and Recent Allies. Each entry uses the same player icon.
- **WoW Ignore** — Uses WoW’s native Ignore feature for eligible contacts. Removing an avoid entry preserves ignores you added yourself.
- **Group Finder filtering** — Hide matching players, groups led by avoided players, and applicants. Turn off hiding to outline matching player and group results in red. Hover over the result to see the saved reason.
- **Group invitations** — Declines incoming invitations from confirmed avoided characters when WoW exposes the inviter and allows the action.

---

## 🎮 How It Works

1. Right-click a supported player and select **Avoid Player**, or enter their first and last name in the avoid tab and click **+**. Enter a reason to save the player.
2. Press **O** and select the red X tab to open the list.
3. Turn off **Hide avoided Group Finder entries** to highlight matches in Blizzard’s Group Finder. Click the X beside a player in your avoid list to remove them. Open Group Finder views update when you add or remove an entry or change the checkbox.

If players apply together, a single exact match can hide or highlight the entire application. Unidentified applicants stay visible. WoW can withhold some applicants before the addon sees them. An Ignore entry created on another character may be removed the next time that character logs in.

---

## 📦 Installation & Setup

1. **Install** Avoided Players Forever through the CurseForge app for your World of Warcraft: Forever installation.
2. **Enable the addon** in the in-game AddOns list.
3. **Open your avoid list** with **O** and select the red X tab.
4. **Set your preference** for whether matching Group Finder entries are hidden or highlighted in red.

The interface supports English and German and follows your game language, with English as the fallback.
