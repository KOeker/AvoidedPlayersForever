# Avoided Players Forever

<img src="Media/Logo.png" alt="Avoided Players Forever logo" width="128">

Keep an account-wide avoid list in World of Warcraft: Forever. Save a reason for each player, use WoW’s built-in Ignore list, and decide how avoided players appear in your Group Finder applicants.

---

## 🛡️ Core Features

- **Account-wide avoid list** — Search by character name, realm, or reason. Your notes are saved locally.
- **Social window integration** — Manage your list from the red X tab alongside Friends and Recent Allies.
- **WoW Ignore** — Uses WoW’s native Ignore feature for eligible contacts. Removing an avoid entry preserves ignores you added yourself.
- **Applicant filtering** — Hide matching applications by default, or show them in red with the saved reason on hover.

---

## 🎮 How It Works

1. Right-click a supported player, friend, or Recent Ally and select **Avoid Player**. Enter a reason to save the player.
2. Press **O** and select the red X tab to open the list.
3. Turn off **Hide avoided applicants** to highlight matches in Blizzard’s Group Finder. Click the X beside a player in your avoid list to remove them.

If players apply together, a single exact match can hide or highlight the entire application. Unidentified applicants stay visible. WoW can withhold some applicants before the addon sees them. An Ignore entry created on another character may be removed the next time that character logs in.

---

## 📦 Installation & Setup

1. **Install** Avoided Players Forever through the CurseForge app for your World of Warcraft: Forever installation.
2. **Enable the addon** in the in-game AddOns list.
3. **Open your avoid list** with **O** and select the red X tab.
4. **Set your preference** for whether matching Group Finder applications are hidden or highlighted in red.

The interface supports English and German and follows your game language, with English as the fallback.
